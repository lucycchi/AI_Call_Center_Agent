## Billing: charged twice (held out, 11 calls)

**Caller asks:** "I think I got charged twice. There are two $1240 charges on my card from you guys."

**Reference answer:** One of the two charges is usually a pending authorization that drops off in 3 to 5 business days. If both charges post, Meridian opens a case with its payment processor.

**Document text it relies on:**

From `faq/customer_faq`, section "Duplicate charges":

> ## Duplicate charges
> If you see two charges, one is usually a pending authorization that drops off in 3–5 business days. If both post, contact us and we will open a case with our payment processor.

## Warranty: repeat repair (held out, 6 calls)

**Caller asks:** "Does the labor warranty cover a repeat repair? You fixed my blower in March and it's doing the same thing."

**Reference answer:** Repairs carry a 1-year labor warranty on the specific part repaired; if the same part fails within a year, the return visit and labor are free.

**Document text it relies on:**

From `warranty/warranty_policy`, section "Repair warranty (Meridian labor warranty)":

> ## Repair warranty (Meridian labor warranty)
> - All repairs performed by Meridian carry a **1-year labor warranty** on the specific part repaired or replaced.
> - If the same part fails within 1 year, the return visit and labor are free. Parts are covered if they were supplied by Meridian.
> - The repair warranty does not cover different components, pre-existing conditions noted on the original invoice, or failures caused by lack of maintenance, power surges, or customer modifications.
>

## Warranty: transfer (held out, 8 calls)

**Caller asks:** "We just bought a house and the previous owner had a system installed by you. Does the warranty transfer?"

**Reference answer:** Manufacturer parts warranties can transfer once to a new homeowner, usually within 90 days of the sale, with a manufacturer transfer fee of $50 to $100. Meridian's labor warranty does not transfer.

**Document text it relies on:**

From `warranty/warranty_policy`, section "Warranty transfer":

> ## Warranty transfer
> - Manufacturer parts warranties may transfer once to a subsequent homeowner, usually within 90 days of the home sale and subject to a manufacturer transfer fee ($50–$100). Meridian can provide the transfer form.
> - Meridian labor warranties do **not** transfer to a new homeowner.
>

## Warranty: claim (working set, 14 calls)

**Caller asks:** "I need to file a warranty claim. The unit you installed last year stopped working."

**Reference answer:** A full system installation has a 2-year Meridian labor warranty, so a unit installed within the last year is covered; a warranty diagnostic is booked and there is no diagnostic fee if the failure is covered.

**Document text it relies on:**

From `warranty/warranty_policy`, section "New system installation warranty":

> ## New system installation warranty
> - Meridian provides a **2-year labor warranty** on all full system installations.
> - Comfort Club members at the time of installation receive an extended **5-year labor warranty**.
> - Manufacturer parts warranties are separate and vary by brand (see table below). Meridian registers every installed unit with the manufacturer within 30 days of install. Registration is required for the full parts warranty term; unregistered units typically receive only 5 years.
>

From `warranty/warranty_policy`, section "Filing a claim":

> ## Filing a claim
> Call to schedule a warranty diagnostic. If the failure is covered, there is no diagnostic fee. If the technician determines the failure is not covered, the standard $89 diagnostic fee applies.

## Glossary

- **Reference answer:** the correct answer, written in advance, that the agent's answer is graded against.
- **Held-out case:** a test case used only for the final measurement, never for tuning.
- **Working-set case:** a test case used while tuning the agent.
- **Evidence span:** the exact lines of a document that support a reference answer.
- **Action gap:** a request the agent hands off because it needs an action on money or membership.
