# Autopay

_Author-supplied policy, 2026-10-21: not one of Meridian's original documents. Added because no document covered
autopay (money and membership actions decision D54)._

## Setting up or changing autopay
Customers can pay invoices and Comfort Club fees automatically by card or bank account (ACH). To set up or change autopay, including replacing an expired card, we send a secure autopay link by text or email to the contact details on the account, valid for 24 hours. The customer enters the payment details and agrees to the autopay terms on that page. We never take card or bank numbers over the phone or in chat. Autopay starts once the form is completed, and we email a confirmation and a copy of the terms.

## When autopay charges
Autopay charges invoices on their due date, monthly Comfort Club plans on the enrollment day each month, and annual plans on the renewal date. We send a reminder 3 days before each charge.

## Turning autopay off
Customers can turn autopay off at any time by phone, chat or the link in any reminder; it stops for charges at least one business day away.
